### Q4. Is the power math sound?

**Arithmetic: correct.**

- Formula: `delta_MDE = (z_{α/2} + z_power) · sd_d / √n` — standard paired normal approximation.
- Bonferroni: `α = 0.05/6` two-sided ⇒ critical tail `0.05/12` ⇒ `z = 2.6383` — verified (`scipy.stats.norm.ppf(1 - 0.05/12) ≈ 2.63826`).
- Worked example: `(2.6383 + 0.8416) · 0.1284 / 100 = 0.004468` — verified.
- 119×: `0.5326 / 0.00447 ≈ 119` — verified.

**Assumptions / honesty:**

| Issue | Ruling |
|---|---|
| Bonferroni over 6 **dependent** cells | **Conservative, not anti-conservative.** Shared dictionary structure, nested `d`, preregistered monotone order ⇒ positive dependence ⇒ true FWER control weaker than Bonferroni implies; MDE is slightly **overstated** (harder to clear significance). Irrelevant to effect-size bar 0.15. Holm or hierarchical testing would be cleaner; not load-bearing. |
| `sd_d` under large-effect alternative | **Disclosed correctly (threat #3).** Under a small true effect, sd can differ. Rows at sd=0.30/0.50 bound the MDE *if* those sds obtain; they do **not** prove the pilot sd transfers to Tier 2. Acceptable for power *planning*; invalid as evidence the real effect is large. |
| Pilot n=1000, single seed | **Inadequate to “fix” sd_d as a population quantity**; adequate only as a rough order-of-magnitude for planning (power threat #4 admits this). Confirmatory needs multi-seed. |
| eps=0.05/0.10 bit-identical to eps=0 | **Expected, not a harness bug.** Same `A`, same test set, l1 already at ceiling; linear arm independent of eps. Those rows carry **no independent information**. No conclusion may lean on them — power states this. |
| Bar 0.15 | **Reverse-engineered from the pilot, not set blind.** Justifications (“33.6× MDE80”, “30× null effect”) are *post hoc numerology* around a number the pilot already clears by ~3.5×. Before pilot, an honest bar for a *theorem demo* would either be “reproduce phase transition within ε” or a `d*`-ratio ≥2× — not “CI lower ≥ 0.15 on the metric that maximizes the gap.” I would **not** have set 0.15 pre-pilot without the 0.53 measurement in hand. |
| FLOP extrapolation | **Sound for FISTA on contended CPU at the measured rate**, as order-of-magnitude. **Does not hold as written if solver becomes LARS/OMP** — different flop structure; 3090 “n=100k free” claim is FISTA-specific and still assumes the whole experiment remains FISTA@250. |

**Power verdict:** math correct; **bar not honest as a pre-registered scientific threshold** — it is a post-pilot clearance number. More importantly, **power answers the wrong question**: free `n` and 119× MDE prove you can re-measure a theorem with absurd precision, which is the defect.
