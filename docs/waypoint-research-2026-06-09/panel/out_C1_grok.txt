## 3. “Ridge−MLP n.s. → linear ceiling” — **FAIL as inference** (absence of evidence)

Headline numbers are fine as **descriptive** estimates. They do **not** support the old paper’s “establishing ~84% as a *linear post-hoc ceiling*.”

| Fact | Implication |
|---|---|
| ΔNDCG ridge−MLP = 0.022 [−0.033, 0.077], Holm p=0.43 | Cannot reject equality |
| CI includes **MLP better by up to 0.033** and ridge better by up to 0.077 | ≈ **−4 to +10 recovery points** on this gap — practically large |
| G2 FAIL (0.089 ≫ 0.015) | Study is underpowered for the ceiling contrast on 60 queries |
| Learning curve: ridge pulls ahead of MLP as n grows (safe panel) | Directional hint only; not a powered equality test |

### Paper may claim
- Point estimates + 95% paired bootstrap CIs on the **stated protocol**.
- Ridge is the **highest observed** among {ridge, procrustes, mlp, c3a} here.
- Ridge ≫ C3a (Holm-significant).
- Ridge vs MLP **not distinguishable** at α=0.05 under this n.
- G2 failed; residual gap is **not** an information-theoretic 16% floor.
- Prefer “**observed plateau under this protocol**” over “ceiling.”

### Paper must **not** claim
- “MLP does not exceed ridge ⇒ linear post-hoc ceiling.”
- “No nonlinear benefit” / “capacity is linear.”
- “~84% is the recoverable upper bound” or “irreducible 16%.”
- G2-powered precision on method contrasts.

Non-significance is **absence of evidence**, not evidence of absence. `paper_edits.md` weakens “ceiling” wording and notes G2 fail, but **does not ban the invalid inference** explicitly.
