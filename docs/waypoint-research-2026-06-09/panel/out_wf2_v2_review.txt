## 2. Is the NEGATIVE real? **Yes.**

### v1 PROMISING and v2 NEGATIVE are consistent (same code path)

On the **exact same 6 v1 cells** and the same `block_loo_analysis`:

| | Dataset ρ | Encoder ρ | Beats both nulls |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| v1 claim | +0.8857 | +0.8286 | yes → PROMISING-BUT-UNDERPOWERED |
| Recompute on v1 subset from frozen features | **+0.885714** | **+0.828571** | **True** |

v2 did not change the metric code or the six reused packs’ metrics. It only expanded the regime set (ArguAna × 3 + e5 × 4). Regime R / margin for the six old cells are byte-stable vs `objA_validation.md`.

### Honest generalization story

| Slice | Spearman(margin, R) |
|---|---:|
| old 6 cells | **+0.886** |
| new 6 cells alone | **−0.943** |
| all 12 | **+0.042** |
| ArguAna 3 | **−1.000** |
| e5 4 | **−0.800** |

Influence (dataset-block LOO, drop one dataset):

| Drop | n | margin ρ |
|---|---:|---:|
| **ArguAna** | 9 | **+0.733** |
| FiQA2018 | 9 | −0.833 |
| NFCorpus | 9 | −0.733 |
| SciFact | 9 | −0.867 |

ArguAna is the **dominant** inversion driver: hold it out and the positive inventory pattern returns. That is not a reason to discard the NEGATIVE — the prereg **registered** ArguAna as a novel dataset block. The point of powered validation was exactly this holdout.

Encoder-side: drop e5 still leaves ρ≈−0.36; e5 is a second, weaker invertor (low R on SciFact/FiQA e5 with tiny positive margins).

**Mechanism (not a bug):** Under dataset LOO, training cells all have **positive** margin means. OLS learns a positive margin→R slope. ArguAna’s **negative** margins then predict low R (~0.42–0.55) while actual R is **high** (~0.75–0.86) — see OOF rows in `objA_v2_validation.json` L49–80. That systematically anti-ranks the novel block.

So: v1 n=6 / 3×2 inventory **overfit** a within-family correlation; on novel dataset+encoder blocks the predictor does **not** generalize and **loses** to gap-only.
