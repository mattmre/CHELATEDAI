## Verdict: **CUT**

Not “probably fails.” As written it is **statistically unable to produce a legitimate POSITIVE** at realistic *n*, and **operator-structurally biased against G** in the exact stratum that defines the claim. Cutting this before GPU is the same class of win as cutting the β-sweep.

---

## A. Rigged-to-WIN

### 1. UNKNOWN estimator — gameable? circular?

**Not circular with G in a “harmonic always wins” sense.**  
`u(q) = mean cosine distance to k=10 FIT docs` is computed in the **frozen store space before correction**, threshold frozen on CAL. That is real, pre-declared, and not outcome-contaminated. Good.

**It does not mechanically favor G.** It does the opposite: UNKNOWN = low local support = **exactly where a FIT-built diffusion graph + Nyström is worst conditioned**. If anything this stratum **selects against** harmonic methods.

**Residual shopping risk (mild, not fatal):**
- 70th-percentile is arbitrary (60th/80th would re-label the set). Locked once = fine; if anyone reopens it after peeking, shopping is trivial.
- Hard queries have higher NDCG variance → noisier Δ → makes **false positives harder**, not easier.
- Secondary “isometry residual” is correctly demoted to descriptive; keep it that way. Promoting it post-hoc would be L4.

**Sanity gate (L worse on UNKNOWN by ≥0.05 on CAL)** is a good REGIME-INVALID escape. It does **not** fix power or Nyström.

### 2. Is L strong enough?

**No. “Best of ridge / orthogonal Procrustes on CAL” is not the strongest linear baseline this program is obliged to beat.**

From D1 on this same swap arena:
- **Ridge already beats Procrustes and residual MLP** (NDCG ~0.681 vs 0.665 vs 0.659 on the literal protocol).
- The honest production baseline in-repo is **leakage-safe affine ridge** `Wx + b` (`AffineRidgeAdapter`), not pure orthogonal Procrustes.
- Orthogonal Procrustes is the **weaker** of the two named arms; “best of two” mostly means “ridge, and we checked Procrustes lost.”

**Strongest linear baseline you must beat (name it and freeze it):**

> **Leakage-safe full-FIT affine ridge (`y ≈ Wx + b`), λ selected on CAL only**,  
> **plus a capacity-matched reduced-rank affine ridge** with rank `r` chosen on CAL over a grid that includes G’s harmonic dimension (and |A| if anchors act as a bottleneck).

Optional honesty add-ons (still linear, still closed-form):  
CCA / PLS with rank on CAL.  
Do **not** let G’s only opponent be under-parameterized global ridge while G gets anchors + eigenmaps + Nyström + a second ridge in harmonic space.

### 3. Parameter / budget “parity”

**Stated parity is aspirational, not real.**

| Side | Free knobs |
|------|------------|
| **L as written** | essentially one λ (or Procrustes with ~0 continuous knobs) |
| **G** | \|A\|, anchor mining rule, graph-k, kernel/bandwidth, #eigenvectors, Nyström recipe, then Procrustes/ridge **inside** harmonic coords |

G has a **multi-dimensional capacity + smoothing stack**. “Same CAL budget” only means same number of trial slots if you actually allocate them that way — the draft does not specify a matched search grid or DoF cap. Without that, a POSITIVE is easily a **capacity win**, not a “nonlinear geometry” win.

### 4. Multiple comparisons / shopping

**Frozen POSITIVE rule is conservative (anti-shopping for wins), not immune to narrative shopping after a NEGATIVE.**

- Dual-dataset same-sign + LB≥0.01 + KNOWN floor + sanity gate → **hard to false-positive**.
- Still two datasets, two strata, two estimators, OPERATOR-UNSTABLE, REGIME-INVALID → lots of **post-hoc story slots** (“almost,” “unstable but promising,” “secondary residual region…”). Those are fine if labeled descriptive; they are not wins.

Primary H1 text is operationally OK. The danger is **soft rescue language**, not the decision inequality itself.

## B. Rigged-to-FAIL (equally disqualifying)

### 5. Low-density / Nyström — unwinnable-by-construction?

**Not in the β=0.10 sense** (oracle gap ≈ 0 ⇒ no method can recover).  
Here the arena still has a large recoverable gap; ridge already closes most of it. Recovery is possible.

**Yes in the operator-structural sense (near-unwinnable for *this* G on *this* stratum):**

1. UNKNOWN is **defined** as low FIT support / high mean distance.
2. Diffusion maps need **local sampling density** for a stable Laplacian spectrum.
3. Nyström out-of-sample extension into a **low-density region of a graph built on FIT** is the textbook failure mode of spectral embeddings.
4. Step 4 of G is “do Procrustes/ridge **in harmonic coordinates**” — so G is a **nonlinear coordinate change + linear map**. Where the coordinate change is poorly identified, G ≈ **worse ridge**.

So: not “no structure exists,” but “**you asked a density-based operator to prove itself on the density-selected hard set after building the geometry only where density is high.**” That is the same *flavor* of design error as the β-screen (protocol selects a regime that defeats the method’s premise), even if the math is different.

**If you refused to CUT and still ran alignment:**
- Build the affinity graph on the **union of paired FIT points in both encoder spaces** (or joint anchor-relative cloud), not FIT-store-only then Nyström into OOD; **and/or**
- Density-adaptive / self-tuning kernel; **and/or**
- Drop Nyström-as-load-bearing and use a method that does not require extending eigenfunctions into sparse regions.

Even with those fixes, power and scope issues remain.

### 6. Power at realistic REPORT-UNKNOWN *n*

**The draft’s “~20–30 UNKNOWN queries” is already optimistic; realistic SciFact-scale is worse.**

| Total queries (style) | REPORT (30%) | EXPECTED UNKNOWN (~30% of REPORT) |
|----------------------|--------------|-------------------------------------|
| 60 (D1 pack) | 18 | **~5–6** |
| 100 (rung-16 Arena A) | 30 | **~9** |
| 300 (full SciFact-ish) | 90 | **~27** |

Extrapolation from **this program’s own** D1 paired contrast (ridge−MLP half-width **0.055 at n=60**):

| UNKNOWN *n* | ≈95% paired half-width | Point estimate needed so **LB ≥ +0.01** |
|------------:|------------------------:|-----------------------------------------:|
| 9 | ~0.14 | **~0.15** |
| 18 | ~0.10 | **~0.11** |
| 27–30 | ~0.08 | **~0.09** |
| 60 | 0.055 | ~0.065 |
| ~200 | ~0.03 | ~0.04 |
| ~460 | ~0.02 | ~0.03 |
| ~820 | ~0.015 | ~0.025 |

To clear a **+0.01 lower bound** with non-laughable power you need either:
- a **heroic true Δ** (≈0.10+ absolute NDCG on the hard stratum alone — larger than rung-16’s entire plane-vs-global effects), or  
- **hundreds of UNKNOWN queries**, not tens.

Rung-16 REPORT CIs already show the regime: plane−global **−0.003** with CI **[−0.051, +0.041]** at *n*=30 full REPORT — half-width ~0.05 **before** subsetting to UNKNOWN.

**Conclusion:** at realistic REPORT-UNKNOWN *n*, the study is **underpowered to ever clear the written POSITIVE bar** unless G’s true effect is absurdly large. That is a **guaranteed fail-closed by design**, not an honest coin-flip. Same class of bug as needing 817 queries for a 0.015 ridge−MLP threshold in D1.

Also: POSITIVE requires **both datasets** same sign with that LB — multiplies the underpowering.

### 7. “G must not lose on KNOWN (Δ_known ≥ −0.005)”

**Conceptually fair** (no robbing Peter).  
**Statistically another random veto** at small *n*: KNOWN has more queries than UNKNOWN, but HW is still large vs 0.005. A true zero on KNOWN can fail −0.005 by noise.

Not the main kill. Soften to “KNOWN not significantly worse” or descriptive only if you ever reopened this design; do not treat it as the headline issue.

## C. Falsifiability and scope

### 8. Does HI-1 test the theory?

**No. It tests a weaker analogy — and that analogy is already dominated by published/verified results.**

| Claim layer | What is proven / known | What HI-1 tests |
|-------------|------------------------|-----------------|
| Superposition / CS wedge | **Within-space** nonlinear readout of *k*-sparse features can beat linear (Garg–Kleinberg–Peng; Engels et al.) | Not this |
| Cross-space frozen alignment | mini-vec2vec: **linear gradient-free ≥ nonlinear adversarial** for unsupervised realignment; vec2vec OOD claim refuted | **This**, with extra spectral machinery |

HI-1 failure would **not** touch the CS theorem (draft admits this).  
HI-1 success would **not** establish the wedge either — it would show a particular spectral coordinate change helped alignment in low-support queries.

**More decisive per GPU-hour:** a **within-space** test:

> In one frozen embedding space with **known superposed / multi-dim features** (synthetic or Engels-style circular features), can a **gradient-free** harmonic / CS-style readout beat a **linear probe** on feature recovery (or on a retrieval label that depends on the nonlinear feature)?

That attacks the *actual* wedge with far less protocol risk, no cross-encoder pack drama, and a metric with more power than NDCG@10 on 9 OOD queries.

### 9. Still-unfalsifiable sentence

H1 as an **operational inequality on REPORT UNKNOWN NDCG** is falsifiable (plus REGIME-INVALID / OPERATOR-UNSTABLE exits).

The protected / unfalsifiable *scientific* move is this (§0 / §8 coupling):

> “HI-1 is therefore explicitly a test of the analogy, and a failure is evidence about *the analogy*, not a refutation of the compressed-sensing theorem.”

Combined with the NEGATIVE write-up framing, **no outcome of HI-1 can falsify the motivating theory**, and a NEGATIVE is pre-narrated as a “sixth hazard” methodology bullet. That is honest about scope — and it is also why spending GPU on HI-1 has low decision value: **you already know how you will interpret both sides.**

Operationally soft phrase inside G:

> “this is the ‘extend the known geometry into the unknown region’ step”

presupposes that “known geometry” **extends**. OPERATOR-UNSTABLE partially covers implementation failure; it does not make the presupposition a hypothesis.

## Bottom line

HI-1 is a well-written **fail-closed theater**: honest prior, good freeze/lock hygiene, good REGIME-INVALID gate — and then a primary stratum that **starves G of density**, a **win bar the paired bootstrap cannot clear at realistic *n***, a baseline that is **not the strongest linear map you already ship**, and a scientific target that is **only an analogy** to the one theorem that made the cell look exciting. The program has already learned, repeatedly, that elaborate structure loses to trivial global linear on this arena; the literature already says linear unsupervised alignment matches or beats nonlinear. Running HI-1 will almost certainly produce a sixth pre-narrated NEGATIVE that teaches nothing you cannot write today without touching the 3090. **Cut it.** If you still want a decisive coin-flip on the deep-research wedge, run the **within-space** gradient-free vs linear probe — that is the only cell that is both winnable in principle and not already dominated.
