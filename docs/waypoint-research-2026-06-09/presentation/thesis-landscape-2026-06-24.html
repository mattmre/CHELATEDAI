03 What we already know prior art

Every individual ingredient of the thesis is published — several landed Feb–May 2026, so the space is hot and converging. These are not ours; they're the baselines and the surrounding field.

Post-hoc adapter on a frozen base

Additive residual MLP on frozen embeddings (new = orig + f(orig)), API-only, supervised. Structurally identical to the chelation adapter family.

Search-Adaptor · ACL 2024 · arXiv:2310.08750

Per-query dimension masking

Query-conditioned dimension selection on a frozen encoder; "Manifold Clustering" hypothesis. Nearly exactly dimension_mask_predictor.py.

DIME · SIGIR 2024  ·  query-aware masking · arXiv:2602.03306

Drift / semantic-shift detection

Formal geometric-concentration statistic for embedding drift — but the remedy is text segmentation, not correction. The detect half is solved; the correct half is the differentiator.

Semantic Shift · arXiv:2603.21437

Index lifecycle under change

Detect degraded partitions → targeted teardown + local re-cluster; cost-model repartitioning; brain-inspired deprioritization. The closest prior art to "disintegration" — but operates on index structure, never embeddings.

Ada-IVF · Quake · FreshDiskANN · SmartVector

Steering-vector banks ("posts")

A bank of steering anchors; steer toward the K-nearest centroid; conditional firing. The literal published form of your steering posts — but a static bank over LLM activations.

SVF · arXiv:2602.01654 · CAST · SADI

Routing over adapter banks

Dynamic MoE/LoRA routing; weighted sums over low-rank atoms; per-passage RAG routing. The one-shot router baseline — all train-once-then-frozen.

LD-MoLE · arXiv:2509.25684 · Queryable LoRA · arXiv:2605.08423

Flow / nonlinear warping

Concept-conditioned velocity field for nonlinear activation transport. Nearest cousin to "annealed routes" — but offline for fixed concepts, not annealed under drift.

FLAS · arXiv:2605.05892

Graph memory + GNN-RAG · disk-resident

Typed dynamic graph memory; GNN retrieval over KGs; near-storage ANN; flash-resident inference. Surrounds the Evidence-DAG and computational-storage directions.

Zep · HippoRAG 2 · GNN-RAG · DiskANN · "LLM in a Flash"

04 What is truly novel — and proven runtime-verified

These four are genuinely new and backed by command-output evidence from the production code path. The first two are the real, citable value of the paper.

finding 1 The C2-oracle hazard

The "obvious" maintenance baseline — re-embed affected docs with the original frozen encoder — is a degenerate oracle for synthetic vector drift: it inverts the perturbation exactly and returns to the pre-drift state. It's an upper bound masquerading as a baseline. Only an encoder/query upgrade defeats it; document-side model-swap does not. A whole class of drift studies could record a tautological "100% recovery" and mistake it for a result.

C2 == C0 at full float precision on BOTH datasets  ·  SciFact 0.006 = 0.006  ·  NFCorpus 0.041 = 0.041  ·  max|baseline − final| = 0

finding 2 The supervision-signal mechanism

An unsupervised homeostatic correction loop has no signal pointing back toward pre-drift geometry, so its loss-optimal correction is near-identity — "do almost nothing." Recovery requires an explicit pre-drift supervision channel (held-out anchor pairs). Same failure class as same-encoder distillation (teacher ≡ student).

Unbounded unsupervised correction norm ≈ 1.9e-4 (≈ identity)  ·  the bounded ~1e-2 is just the bound floor, not learning  ·  synthetic regime: correction_applied = 0/36, store byte-identical

apparatus Reproducible harness + the encoder-upgrade arena

Seeded deterministic drift injector with JSON-manifest reproducibility, a recovery-trajectory metric suite, and a pre-registered protocol. The encoder-upgrade arena (queries move to a new encoder; cached doc vectors stay in old space) is the design that defeats the oracle and gives the loop a real target.

Catastrophic drift reproduced: SciFact 0.819 → 0.006 · NFCorpus 0.598 → 0.041  ·  oracle ceiling: 0.813 / 0.612  ·  2 datasets × 12 cycles × seeds {42,1337,7}

result Supervised closed-loop partial recovery

With the oracle defeated and InfoNCE supervision provided, the supervised bounded adapter actually engages and recovers a measurable fraction of catastrophic drift. Honest qualifier: recovery scales with budget on SciFact but inverts on NFCorpus (more steps hurt); unbounded beats bounded on SciFact but ties on NFCorpus.

Actuator fires 12/12 cycles · 3/3 seeds · both datasets · C3a > C0 every seed  ·  SciFact C3a 0.159 / C4a 0.206  ·  NFCorpus C3a 0.054 / C4a 0.054  ·  budget effect dataset-dependent

05 Novel but unproven — the speculative core not built

This is the heart of the thesis, and it is where the defensible long-term novelty lives. None of it is implemented. The paper uses a single bounded MLP adapter — none of the structures below.

Living bank of steering posts gap #1

A bank of anchor vectors over retrieval pools with a drift-driven lifecycle: posts pruned when detectors fire, then re-annealed. No published steering system manages the bank as a living structure — all are static. This is the headline uncovered novelty.

your terms: guidance posts + disintegration + annealing

Annealed routes as trajectories gap #3

Multi-hop learned paths through the bank where the path itself is the annealed object (exploratory → stable), with conditional branching — which post you visit depends on where you are.

your terms: annealed routes + query-conditioned subspaces

Correction-geometry meta-space

Model the space of corrections as a structured manifold (congruent/parallel subspaces, invariant intercepts) that persists across base-model swaps so only coordinates re-sync. Most conceptually novel of the five concepts; least developed. Testable kernel: do learned correction subspaces transfer across a model swap with only cheap re-alignment?

vs Procrustes / BCT / task-arithmetic — comparison not yet run

Evidence DAG with actuator nodes

A typed query ↔ doc ↔ correction graph (adapters/masks/routes as nodes) with drift-triggered structural pruning, evolving toward a GNN that must beat a flat-pool baseline. Prior-art comparison (Zep, HippoRAG 2) not yet adversarially verified.

Phase II steps 12 + 15

Index ↔ correction coupling

Pool lifecycle coupled to embedding-correction training under one temperature controller — the unification Ada-IVF/Quake (index-only) and Search-Adaptor (correction-only) never combine. Weakest novelty form (combination) — only counts with end-to-end evidence.

your term: self-annealing lattice

The proving experiment designed, not run

An annealed-route bank recovers retrieval where a frozen SVF-style static bank degrades AND a one-shot LD-MoLE router degrades. The baselines are handed to us; the head-to-head that would prove the steering-post thesis has not been executed.

slots into Phase II step 14, next to Ada-IVF/Quake

06 The honest scorecard

Every number below was re-verified against the run manifests on 2026-06-24. The headline is real but modest — read the ceiling, not the multiplier.

Read this, not the "25×": SciFact's "~25× over frozen" sounds huge only because C0 is ≈ 0.006 (near-zero). The honest framing is fraction of the oracle gap closed: 19% on SciFact, 2.25% on NFCorpus. It's a real partial recovery with a characterized ceiling — never full recovery.
The "12-cycle loop" is one-shot. Every trajectory is a flat step function — the correction is fully applied at cycle 1 and re-applied identically for 11 more. Operationally it's a one-shot supervised realignment, not an iterating loop.
Budget sensitivity inverts. More training steps help on SciFact (→0.182) but hurt on NFCorpus (30 steps best, →0.041 at 2000). Anchor-overfitting on the broader query distribution — a flagged open question.

07 What can honestly be presented today

Framed as a methodology / harness paper — a reusable framework + the oracle-baseline catch + a worked partial example — not "closed loop recovers drift." This is defensible right now, on two datasets.

① A reproducible drift-recovery evaluation framework

Seeded deterministic drift injector with manifest reproducibility, a recovery-trajectory metric suite, and a pre-registered protocol on two BEIR datasets. Releasable so others avoid the same traps.

② The two hazards (the citable core)

The C2-oracle catch + the supervision-signal mechanism. Both are general evaluation-design lessons that outlive this prototype — the strongest writable assets.

③ The encoder-upgrade arena + an honest mixed/partial positive

The design that defeats the oracle, plus a worked example where a supervised loop engages (12/12, 3/3 seeds, both datasets) and recovers a measurable, dataset-dependent fraction — explicitly never closing the gap to the oracle.

Not presentable as proven: the post-bank, annealed routes, the lifecycle, the meta-space, the DAG/GNN — and not "the closed loop recovers drift" (it partially does, in one arena, as a one-shot). Keep those in "future work / pre-registered."

09 Honest caveats brutal honesty

!
Recovery is partial with a characterized ceiling, not full. SciFact closes ~19% of the oracle gap; NFCorpus ~2.25%. The "25×" is large only because C0 ≈ 0.006.
!
The 12-cycle "closed loop" is a flat step function — operationally a one-shot supervised realignment, not an iterating loop.
!
In the separate synthetic-drift regime the loop never engaged (0/36, store byte-identical). That's an evaluation-design finding, not a recovery capability.
!
The paper proves exactly one of three deep-dive gaps (the retrieval-pool domain) and does it with the established shim family (a single bounded near-identity adapter). The two gaps that are the defensible novelty — living-bank lifecycle and routes-as-trajectories — are absent.
!
Concept-3 (Evidence DAG), Concept-4 (correction geometry), Concept-5 (computational storage) prior-art claims were never adversarially vote-verified — treat as unassessed, not confirmed-open.
!
The single adversarially-confirmed genuinely-open component is bounded / INT8-safe correction budgets (BoundedAdapter) — confirmed via a 0–3 refutation that Search-Adaptor already bounds corrections.
!
"More flexible than matrix multiplication" needs precision: chained linear transforms are still linear. Flexibility comes only from nonlinearity between hops or conditional branching — fiber-bundle language without theorems reads as decoration.
