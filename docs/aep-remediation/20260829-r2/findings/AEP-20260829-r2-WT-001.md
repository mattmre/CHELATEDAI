## Defect evidence and commands

If the final `communicate()` also times out, it raises `ChildExecutionFailure` without proving `process.poll()` is terminal, reaping the process, or proving the process group exited. `run_supervised` immediately calls `restore_and_verify` in `finally`. ## Root cause and atomic fix steps

The supervisor equates “KILL was sent” with “the whole process group is terminal and reaped.”

1. Make an explicit terminal-and-reaped child/group state a precondition for service restoration.
2. After the final wait timeout, run a bounded platform-specific process-group liveness and reap check; retain PID/PGID evidence.
3. If termination cannot be proven, keep the service stopped, emit a private operator recovery record, quarantine only supervisor-owned paths without following aliases, and refuse publication.
4. Add a real TERM-ignoring subprocess-group test plus a fake `returncode=None` test; neither may call restoration before terminal proof.
