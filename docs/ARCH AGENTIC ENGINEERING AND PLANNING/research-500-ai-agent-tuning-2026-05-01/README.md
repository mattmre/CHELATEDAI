# 500-Item AI Model And Agent Tuning Research Run

Date: 2026-05-01

## Objective

Analyze 500 white papers, studies, technical reports, benchmark papers, and high-signal engineering articles related to AI model tuning, agent tuning, model-scope steering, engine-scope control, evaluation, and self-improvement. Map findings to ChelatedAI's current engine and model-scope layers, then identify the best 50 additions or realignments for near-term implementation.

## Method

EVOKORE-MCP resolved the applicable workflow as the Panel of Experts framework. The research synthesis therefore follows:

1. Discovery across authoritative sources.
2. Mechanism extraction.
3. Mapping to ChelatedAI layers.
4. Critique and failure-mode review.
5. Matrix comparison.
6. Feasibility posture.
7. Ranked backlog extraction.
