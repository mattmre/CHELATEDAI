## Abstract & Honest Claim Boundary

Upgrading a dense retrieval embedding model in production traditionally forces either full-corpus re-embedding (expensive, slow, and write-amplifying) or brittle linear query translators that degrade severely under narrow-cone encoder anisotropy (e.g., BGE, E5) and explode spectrally across multi-generation encoder upgrades.

We present and empirically validate a **zero-training, qrels-free, closed-form spherical migration stack** packaged in `spherical_migration_bridge.py` and gated by a fail-closed typed representation ABI (`TRA-1`). Across **six BEIR benchmarks** (`SciFact`, `NFCorpus`, `FiQA2018`, plus unseen `ArguAna`, `SCIDOCS`, and `Quora`) and a **five-encoder / four-hop lineage chain** (`E0` MiniLM-384 → `E1` BGE-small-384 → `E2` E5-small-384 → `E3` MPNet-768 → `E4` BGE-base-768), the packaged stack delivers three verified positive results:

1. **Single-Hop Zero-Write Alignment (`A3` + `#355` CSLS):** Centered Shrinkage-Whitened (CORAL) Spherical Procrustes (`A3`) outperforms tuned Ridge regression on **17/18** domain × swap cells (`A3+CSLS` beats `Ridge+CSLS` on **17/18**) and rescues narrow-cone collapse relative to uncentered Procrustes on **18/18** cells (up to `+0.2199` nDCG@10 on `FiQA2018 E3→E4`).
2. **Multi-Hop Lineage Composition (`A2c` Conformal Procrustes):** Adding exact zero-hyperparameter spherical-cap radius matching ($s = \|Y_c\|_F / \|X_c\|_F$) to Centered Orthogonal Procrustes yields a conformal orthogonal map $y = s((x - \mu_x) Q) + \mu_y$ that composes associatively while holding exact spectral condition number $\kappa(s Q) = 1.00$ across arbitrary hops. Across all **6 BEIR domains × 4 hops**, `A2c` achieves a **24/24 sweep** over composed tuned Ridge (whose condition number explodes to $1.18\times 10^{12}$–$5.49\times 10^{12}$), curing the narrow-cone Hop-2 (`E2→E1→E0`) dip by `+0.029` to `+0.182` nDCG@10 over unscaled `A2`.
3. **Partial-Backfill Score Fusion (`B1/B3`) & Fail-Closed Interlock (`TRA-1`):** Monotone empirical-CDF quantile calibration combined with Exposure × Spherical-Distortion backfill ordering beats Night-1 Ridge+Z-Score+FastFill on **16/18** cells at 20% backfill budget (up to `+0.0716` nDCG@10), while hardened `TRA-1` blocks **8/8** hostile wrong-space mutations (`0` wrong-space executions).

## 1. Problem Formulation

Consider a production vector index storing $N$ document embeddings $D^{(0)} \in \mathbb{S}^{d_0 - 1}$ generated by a retired encoder $E_0$. When a new query encoder $E_m$ ($m \ge 1$) is deployed—either directly ($E_1 \to E_0$) or after a sequence of $m$ model upgrades ($E_m \to E_{m-1} \to \dots \to E_0$) where $E_0$ weights are no longer available to re-embed queries—three geometric failure modes arise:

1. **Narrow-Cone Anisotropy (1st- and 2nd-Moment Mismatch):** Contrastive encoders such as `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5` (`E1`), `intfloat/e5-small-v2` (`E2`), and `BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5` (`E4`) concentrate embeddings in a tight spherical cap with high mean norm $\|\mu\|_2$ and small centered radius $r = \mathbb{E}[\|x - \mu\|_2]$, whereas isotropic encoders like `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` (`E0`) and `all-mpnet-base-v2` (`E3`) fill the sphere broadly. Measured directly on `SciFact`:
   - `E0` (MiniLM-384): $\|\mu\|_2 = 0.362$, centered radius $r = 0.930$
   - `E1` (BGE-small-384): $\|\mu\|_2 = 0.793$, centered radius $r = 0.608$
   - `E2` (E5-small-384): $\|\mu\|_2 = 0.899$, centered radius $r = 0.437$
   - `E3` (MPNet-768): $\|\mu\|_2 = 0.359$, centered radius $r = 0.932$
   - `E4` (BGE-base-768): $\|\mu\|_2 = 0.761$, centered radius $r = 0.647$
   Uncentered orthogonal Procrustes fails catastrophically whenever source and target cones differ (`0.1142`–`0.4558` nDCG@10). Even *centered* unscaled Procrustes fails when mapping ultra-narrow `E2` ($r=0.437$) into wide `E0` ($r=0.930$), because adding an unscaled target mean $\mu_{E_0}$ overwhelms the centered query signal $(x - \mu_{E_2}) Q$ by a factor of $0.930 / 0.437 \approx 2.13\times$.
2. **Multi-Hop Spectral Collapse:** Multiplying unconstrained Ridge translators $W_{\text{comp}} = W_{m \to m-1} \cdots W_{1 \to 0}$ compounds anisotropic singular-value shrinkage, driving $\kappa(W_{\text{comp}}) \sim 10^{12}$ by Hop 2–4 and destroying retrieval precision.
3. **Mixed-Space Backfill Scale Mismatch:** During incremental background re-embedding (e.g., 10–20% of documents backfilled into $E_{\text{new}}$), raw cosine similarities in a narrow-cone space (`E1`, centered around `0.75–0.90`) systematically dominate bridged similarities in an isotropic space (`E0`, centered around `0.25–0.55`). Even linear Z-score calibration fails when score tail shapes are skewed, causing partial backfill to *degrade* nDCG@10 relative to pure zero-write bridging.

## 3. Packaged Closed-Form Mathematical Architecture

All five production components live in `spherical_migration_bridge.py` with zero iterative GPU training:

### 3.1 Manifold-Precipitated Anchor Selection (`P6` Spherical k-Means++, `P4` k-Center)
Given L2-normalized anchor pool $V \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times d}$ (capped deterministically at $8{,}192$ candidates for large corpora):
- **`select_spherical_kmeanspp_anchors` (`P6`):** Samples $k$ anchors via deterministic $D^2$ angular seeding with chordal distance $d^2(u, v) = \max(0, 2 - 2 u^\top v)$, maximizing coverage across multimodal semantic lobes without selecting isolated outliers.
- **`select_kcenter_anchors` (`P4`):** Trims the bottom 2% cosine-to-centroid outliers, then runs farthest-point traversal on the sphere.

### 3.2 Conformal Radius-Matched Centered Orthogonal Procrustes (`A2c`)
For matched anchor matrices $X \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times d_{\text{in}}}$ and $Y \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times d_{\text{out}}}$, let $\mu_x = \frac{1}{n}\sum_i x_i$, $\mu_y = \frac{1}{n}\sum_i y_i$, $X_c = X - \mathbf{1}\mu_x^\top$, and $Y_c = Y - \mathbf{1}\mu_y^\top$. Computing the thin SVD $X_c^\top Y_c = U \Sigma V^\top$ yields:
$$Q = U V^\top, \qquad s = \frac{\|Y_c\|_F}{\max(\|X_c\|_F, 10^{-12})}, \qquad f(x) = \operatorname{norm}_2\!\Big(s (x - \mu_x) Q + \mu_y\Big)$$

**Exact Associative Multi-Hop Composition (`compose_centered_bridges`):** Given a chain of $m$ bridges $\{( \mu_{x,k}, \mu_{y,k}, Q_k, s_k )\}_{k=1}^m$ mapping $E_m \to \dots \to E_0$ in ambient tangent coordinates (applying L2 projection once at the end), sequential composition collapses into a single exact conformal-orthogonal map:
$$Q_{\text{comp}} = Q_1 Q_2 \cdots Q_m, \qquad s_{\text{comp}} = \prod_{k=1}^m s_k, \qquad \mu_{y,\text{comp}}^{(k)} = s_k (\mu_{y,\text{comp}}^{(k-1)} - \mu_{x,k}) Q_k + \mu_{y,k}$$

**Spectral Condition Guarantee:** Because $s_k > 0$ is a scalar and every $Q_k$ has orthonormal columns/rows (all nonzero singular values identically $1.0$), every same-dimension composition satisfies $\kappa(s_{\text{comp}} Q_{\text{comp}}) = \frac{s_{\text{comp}}}{s_{\text{comp}}} = 1.0000$ identically—and even across a dimension-changing hop ($768 \to 384$, where $Q_{\text{comp}} \in \mathbb{R}^{768 \times 384}$ has orthonormal columns), all $384$ nonzero singular values equal $s_{\text{comp}}$, preserving exact $\kappa = 1.0000$ across all 4 hops!

### 3.3 Shrinkage-Whitened (CORAL) Spherical Procrustes (`A3`)
For single-hop zero-write bridging where 2nd-order covariance anisotropy dominates, `WhitenedSphericalProcrustesBridge` computes shrinkage-regularized covariances with scalar shrinkage $\lambda \in [0, 1]$ (default $\lambda = 0.20$):
$$\Sigma_{\text{reg}}(Z) = (1 - \lambda)\frac{Z^\top Z}{n - 1} + \lambda \frac{\operatorname{tr}(\Sigma)}{d} I_d$$
Using symmetric eigendecomposition $\Sigma_{\text{reg}}^{-1/2}, \Sigma_{\text{reg}}^{+1/2}$ (eigenvalue floor $10^{-8}$), we solve orthogonal Procrustes in whitened coordinates:
$$U \Sigma V^\top = \operatorname{SVD}\!\big((X_c \Sigma_{x,\text{reg}}^{-1/2})^\top (Y_c \Sigma_{y,\text{reg}}^{-1/2})\big), \quad Q = U V^\top, \quad W = \Sigma_{x,\text{reg}}^{-1/2} Q \Sigma_{y,\text{reg}}^{+1/2}$$
$$g(x) = \operatorname{norm}_2\!\big((x - \mu_x) W + \mu_y\big)$$
This aligns both the 1st moment (cone centroid) and 2nd moment (anisotropic covariance ellipsoid) in closed form, with bounded condition number.

### 3.4 Hubness Mitigation (`#355` CSLS) & Quantile-CDF Backfill (`B1/B2/B3`)
- **CSLS (`csls_k=10`):** Subtracting half the mean top-$k$ development-query similarity suppresses hubness bias in bridged dense spaces.
- **Empirical-CDF Quantile Score Fusion (`score_quantile_calibrated_backfill`, `B1`):** Maps native scores $s_{\text{new}}(q, d)$ for backfilled documents through the empirical CDF estimated on unlabeled development queries into the bridged score quantile distribution $F_{\text{mapped}}^{-1}(F_{\text{native}}(s_{\text{new}}))$, eliminating non-linear cone skew.
- **Exposure × Spherical-Distortion Backfill Prioritization (`B2/B3`):** Ranks documents for incremental backfill by blending FastFill exposure rank ($50\%$) with qrels-free spherical distortion $1 - \langle \hat{d}_i, d_i^{\text{old}} \rangle + 0.25\, \mathrm{KL}(P_{\text{new}}(i) \| P_{\text{mapped}}(i))$ over top-64 anchor neighbors ($50\%$).

## 5. Related Falsification: Science Runner-Up `IQ-01` (`20261121-tandem-r1/iq01`)

To check whether pre-outcome matrix geometry can predict adapter composition harm without retrieval evaluation, `scripts/run_iq01_commutator_falsifier.py` (`manifest_sha256 = 7467ab06812597330bbbac99e4c379334693a7b5ed9b8cac638cda573d5a6fc7`, `39.31s`) evaluated **168 real ordered adapter compositions** (56 ordered pairs × 3 BEIR datasets):
- **Order asymmetry** (`|nDCG(A∘B) - nDCG(B∘A)|`, max `0.0300–0.0381`): Commutator norm `||AB - BA||_F` reaches Spearman $\rho = +0.5988$ (SciFact), $+0.7088$ (NFCorpus), $+0.7542$ (FiQA2018) vs. trivial Frobenius deviation `||A-I||_F + ||B-I||_F` ($+0.6240, +0.6672, +0.6957$).
- **Composition harm** (`min(nDCG(A), nDCG(B)) - nDCG(A∘B)`): Trivial Frobenius deviation ($+0.3653, +0.7154, +0.6749$) **beats or ties** commutator norm ($+0.3184, +0.7057, +0.6875$) and decisively outperforms both Henrici non-normality ($+0.2243, +0.3706, +0.4207$) and spectral condition number ($+0.0898, +0.5533, +0.5098$).
- **Decision:** Keep commutator norm only as an optional order-sensitivity diagnostic; do **not** ship `IQ-01` as a harm predictor.
