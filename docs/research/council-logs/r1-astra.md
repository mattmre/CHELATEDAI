## 1. SUPERSEDE policy and exact incremental WFS

### Replacement authority needs an explicit scope

Adopt **direct supersession for ordinary claims** and **transitive supersession for a declared version lineage**. A version-lineage edge must identify the same tenant, entity, single-valued slot and lineage, and carry authority to replace versions in that lineage. Its existence is a durable assertion about version history. Its issuer’s factual claim can subsequently be refuted. This distinction resolves the Astra–Neon disagreement without inferring ancestry from text, timestamps, a common value, or the mere presence of `REFUTE+SUPERSEDE`.

At a fixed snapshot, write \(D_i\) for mandatory validity parents, \(J_i\subseteq D_i\) for parents whose **currency** the child needs, and \(R_i\) for authorized refuters. Use the finite normal program

\[
V_i\leftarrow\bigwedge_{p\in D_i}V_p,\ \bigwedge_{r\in R_i}\operatorname{not}V_r,
\qquad
C_i\leftarrow V_i,\ \operatorname{not}B_i,\ \bigwedge_{p\in J_i}C_p.
\]

For an ordinary direct replacement \(j\to i\), \(B_i\leftarrow V_j\). For declared lineages, apply the same blocker to strict ancestors reached through visible, authorized lineage edges. A definitely valid replacement blocks, an unresolved replacement makes currency unknown in the absence of a definite blocker, and a refuted replacement exerts no force of its own. Compute lineage ancestry signals on the SCC condensation DAG by propagating “definite ancestor” and “possible ancestor” flags. Materializing every ancestor pair would make a length-\(n\) chain quadratic.

| Witness | Required reader behavior |
|---|---|
| `C SUP B`, `B SUP A`, all valid | Only `C` is current under either policy. Obsolescence of `B` preserves its validity. |
| `C REF B`, `B SUP A` | `B` loses replacement force. `A` becomes current, subject to other evidence. A refutation by `C` alone makes no replacement claim over `A`. |
| `C REF+SUP B`, `B SUP A` | With ordinary edges, `A` and `C` are current. Distinct values in one single-valued slot produce abstention with both witnesses. With declared version-lineage edges, `C` covers `A` through `B`, and only `C` is current. An explicit authorized `C SUP A` also resolves the ordinary case. |
| `B` mutually refuted with `X`, `B SUP A` | `V_A=T`, `C_A=U` absent a definite blocker. A reader must not return `A` as settled. |
| `A SUP B SUP A`, or `A SUP A` | Reject cycle-forming ordinary version writes with an explicit receipt. For imported history preserve the records, mark the temporal conflict and abstain for that slot. A quarantined member supplies at most possible replacement force externally. A separately authorized replacement covering the affected versions can resolve them. |
| `D COPY_OF A`, `B SUP A` | The copy inherits `A`’s lost currency. The original and copy remain available to historical queries. |
| `B AMENDS A`, `B SUP A` | `B` requires \(V_A\), while its currency is independent of \(C_A\). `B` can be current. An explicitly declared `DERIVES_CURRENT A` instead makes `B` ineligible once `A` loses currency. |
| `A` and `B` depend positively on one another, `A REF C` | With one conjunctive rule per node and no independent foundation, `A,B` are unfounded and `C` survives. Preserve an `UNFOUNDED` reason distinct from an observed refutation. |

### The rank shield works only while its certificates remain current

Here is the smallest counterexample I found to updating rank metadata only when displayed labels change. Arrows below are `REFUTE`.

1. Start with `B→A` and `C→A`. Both attackers are true at Fitting rank 0. `A` is false at rank 1.
2. Insert `A→B`. The three truth labels stay the same. **`B`’s rank rises from 0 to 2** because its proof now requires `A` to be false.
3. Delete `C→A`. A stale counter accepts `B` as an earlier independent shield for `A`. The new graph contains an unsupported mutual refutation. Its WFS is `A=U, B=U, C=T`.

Across the three-node graph space, **56,568 directed edits preserved all WFS labels while changing at least one minimum Fitting rank**. A two-edit probe that kept old ranks after such an edit produced **48 erroneous second-step skips**, including 12 using only refutation edges. This directly affects production code that drives downstream maintenance from `state_changed` alone. [Carbon’s rank proposal](/D:/GITHUB/CHELATEDAI-STAGE0-NIGHT1/docs/research/council-logs/xc-carbon.md:47) · [Current label-only differential](/D:/GITHUB/CHELATEDAI-STAGE0-NIGHT1/iq04_temporal_evidence.py:940)

**Revised algorithm.** Keep raw Fitting certificates separate from WFS truth. A raw true node has rank zero for an empty body, otherwise one plus the maximum rank of its satisfied literals. A raw false node has one plus the minimum rank of a falsifying literal. Raw unresolved nodes have rank infinity, including nodes that become decidable only through unfounded-set elimination.

For a single refutation edit at head \(w\), allow a fast cut when a surviving falsifying witness has finite rank below \(r(w)\), or when the edited literal comes from an independently certified false attacker of lower rank. Require the edited body to retain the same raw truth and earliest certificate rank. Update edge and witness membership even on a cut. Strict rank is a **sufficient** independence certificate. An unrelated witness at a higher rank can be independent as well. On an uncertain cut, a dependency mutation, or an atomic multi-head rewind, seed all changed heads and traverse the union of old and new influence edges.

Within that region, freeze the incoming boundary. Collapse every cyclic mandatory-positive SCC to false, propagate false through mandatory dependents, and run three-valued propagation on the remainder. This computes WFS for the single-conjunctive-rule class: any remaining unfounded set of unresolved atoms would require every member to depend positively on another member of the set, contradicting acyclicity. Separately recompute raw proof ranks from certificate arrivals, including unchanged boundary facts at their original ranks. Alternative `SUPPORT` proofs would change the rule class and require another proof.

**Complexity verdict.** The semantic rebuild costs \(O(|D|+|E_{\mathrm{incident}}(D)|)\) with indexed adjacency. Include rank-only changes, old edges, both directions of boundary bookkeeping, and emitted index changes in the work measure. Earliest ranks can be rebuilt in level order with a queue and ordered boundary arrivals. A heap implementation must charge its logarithmic factor. A linear implementation needs a bounded-integer bucket/radix strategy or an equivalent ordered index, with its initialization costs counted. Account separately for locating intervals changed by a rewind. The experiments establish exactness for the stated solver. They do not establish a bound in the number of changed answer labels, constant-time arbitrary rewinds, or a production latency figure. ## 2. Stratified K-axis ordinal stabilization

**Build the K=2 logical kernel and expose K=4 as the production engine contract.** K=6 and K=8 are useful specifications for extensions. Their extra coordinates should enter the production evaluator only when their inputs and falsifiers exist.

| Contract | Evaluation order |
|---|---|
| **K=2** | On a supplied authorized snapshot, evaluate epistemic WFS, then temporal currency and current-inheriting dependencies. |
| **K=4** | **Bitemporal visibility → authority-qualified relations → epistemic WFS → temporal currency.** Visibility applies to evidence, edges, grants and revocations. |
| **K=6** | Add context/jurisdiction and defeasible specificity. Intersect visible tenant/entity/jurisdiction scope first. Within authorized scope, apply a declared strict specificity order to comparable defaults and exceptions before constructing the effective defeat relation. Incomparable conflicting exceptions remain unresolved. |
| **K=8** | Add integrity and bounded ranking. Verify the bytes and provenance needed to trust records or grants before using them. Apply the representation ABI again at query routing. Rank only records already admitted for the requested slot, with a frozen query/cutoff and a stable final ID tie-break. |

**Authority precedes WFS; visibility precedes effective authority.** Suppose a low-authority summary refutes a signed primary receipt in a slot where that summary has no defeat authority. Constructing WFS over the raw edge can already suppress the primary record. Sorting by authority afterward cannot recover it. Conversely, applying a grant that becomes visible tomorrow would let a historical query use future knowledge. Snapshot the grant together with the action it authorizes. Use slot- and capability-specific authority: a user correction can be authoritative about a preference without conferring authority over an instrument reading.

A visible equal-or-higher-authority `B REF A` enters the program as `not V_B` in `A`’s body. If an independently valid and authorized `C REF B` grounds `B` as false, `B` loses its effect on `A`. Equal-authority mutual refutation can remain unknown. This handles a refuted authority-bearing claim while keeping the authority comparison itself independent of the truth labels it helps determine.

**Stratified stabilization theorem.** Fix a finite, versioned snapshot and query. Let strata form a finite DAG. For every stratum \(i\), suppose that fixed predecessor outputs \(p_i\) select a deterministic canonical result \(F_i(p_i)\). Sufficient instances include a monotone operator on a finite-height lattice **with least-fixed-point semantics specified**, a finite ground normal program with canonical WFS, and a deterministic pure projection. Require downstream strata to have no write-back into their predecessors. Then evaluating in any topological order gives the same tuple of canonical outputs. A lexicographic decision order over the resulting coordinates ensures a lower-priority coordinate cannot reverse a decision in a higher one.

*Proof.* Every source has a specified canonical output. Induct on topological depth. All predecessors of a node have the same outputs in every permitted order, so the node receives the same input and selects the same canonical result. Finite depth completes the induction. For incremental evaluation, an unchanged stratum input preserves its result. Applying the same argument to affected old/new dependency regions gives the batch result. If each affected evaluator has a linear local-maintenance algorithm, total work is \(O(\sum_i(|D_i|+|E_{\mathrm{incident},i}|))\), plus view lookup, ordering, output, and any separately charged ranking work. Fixed \(K\) absorbs a constant number of passes. **Stratification alone proves neither linear work nor uniqueness of every mathematical fixed point.**

Three counterexamples delimit the theorem. The monotone Boolean identity has two fixed points until a selection convention is supplied. Making authority depend on the claim’s current admission can encode \(A\leftarrow\operatorname{not}A\), producing an unresolved WFS value and synchronous two-cycle. A bounded continuous feedback score \(x_{t+1}=(1+x_t)/2\) started at zero never reaches its limit in finitely many steps. A pure final score evaluated once avoids that problem. Likewise, a one-edge change at the root of a length-\(n\) mandatory chain can change all \(n\) answers. Lexicographic order governs decisions; the information order governs WFS iteration. ### A2g specification and falsifiers

For unit source observations, take \(p_x=\bar x/\|\bar x\|\), with analogous \(p_y\). These are normalized **extrinsic** means. With \(c=\operatorname{clip}(x^\top p_x,-1,1)\), \(h=x-cp_x\), set
\[
\theta=\arccos c=\operatorname{atan2}(\|h\|,c),\qquad
\log_{p_x}x=\frac{\theta}{\|h\|}h.
\]
Use the continuous zero limit at the pole and reject the antipodal ambiguity. Represent tangent vectors in orthonormal frames of dimensions \(d_x-1,d_y-1\). Fit the retained partial Procrustes factor \(Q=U_rV_r^\top\) from the paired tangent cross-product. Calibrate
\[
s_\theta=\frac{\operatorname{RMS}_{\rm cal}\|\log_{p_y}y\|}
                  {\operatorname{RMS}_{\rm cal}\|(\log_{p_x}x)Q\|},
\qquad
\operatorname{Exp}_{p_y}(w)=\cos\|w\|\,p_y+
                 \frac{\sin\|w\|}{\|w\|}w.
\]
Here \(w\) is expressed back in the target tangent plane. Use `atan2` and a stable sinc implementation. Fit poles and \(Q\) on FIT, estimate radius on disjoint CAL with representative source **and target** distributions, select on DEV/SELECT, then lock REPORT. A shrinkage-whitened tangent map \(W_x^{-1/2}QW_y^{1/2}\) is a separate ablation and needs its own projected-energy denominator. The normalized extrinsic pole need not center the tangent logs. A fitted tangent intercept would also be a distinct model.

Gauss’s lemma explains why radial and transverse changes are orthogonal. It does not make angular scaling globally conformal. Under a tangent isometry the radial stretch is \(s\), while transverse stretch at angle \(\theta\) is \(\sin(s\theta)/\sin\theta\). At \(s=1.8,\theta=.9\), these are **1.8 and 1.2751**. Projection can discard additional tangent directions.

Two in-memory controls distinguish when this matters. On a sphere with a known angular dilation \(1.8\), fitted at angles `.10,.35,.65,.95` and tested at `.18,.41,.76,.89` radians in four symmetric tangent directions, A2g’s mean held-out angular error was **below \(10^{-12}\) degrees**. Extrinsic radius-matched A2c gave **5.2653°**. On a balanced identity dataset \(\{\pm e_1,\pm e_2,\pm e_3\}\), A2c had zero error and the A2g pole was undefined. The first fixture establishes a real advantage under the proposed generative law. The second establishes a real failure mode.

Record pole resultant length and split-half angular stability, cut-locus mass, retained rank, projected calibration energy, angular residuals and the fraction with \(\|w\|\ge\pi-\epsilon\). Reject a degenerate fit; route individual out-of-contract queries to a separately validated fallback with a receipt. A narrow-to-wide cone is a plausible angular-dilation test. For unit observations \(r^2=1-\|\bar x\|^2\), so \(r=.44\) and \(r=.93\) imply resultants about `.898` and `.368`. Those two moments do not establish an angular law. A 384→4096 lift also cannot recover target directions absent from source information.

There is one important composition exception. When an intermediate output pole and the next input pole coincide, frames are consistently transformed, and every intermediate norm stays inside the injectivity radius, \(\log_p(\operatorname{Exp}_p w)=w\). The tangent maps then compose exactly. Different poles or wrap-around generally require sequential evaluation. Measure sequential p50/p95 against collapsed affine A2c at batch one on the named hardware. **0.05 ms is a proposed relay target, not measured A2g latency.**

## 4. Harmonized PM-1 through PM-5

**The clean margin and the strength of evidence for that margin are different promises.** Let \(D_i\in\{-1,0,1\}\) be a paired correctness difference, \(q=P(|D_i|=1)\), and \(\delta=E[D_i]\). Then \(\mathrm{SE}(\hat\delta)=\sqrt{(q-\delta^2)/N}\) under independent items. At \(N=500\), 12 wins and 12 losses give \(\hat\delta=0\) and an approximate two-sided 95% lower endpoint of **−1.92 pp**. At 5% discordance, **1,921** independent clean items give an expected 95% half-width of one point. Approximately **3,925** give 80% power for a lower bound above −1 pp when the true effect is zero. The cited 400–800 estimate does not support the latter claim. Conversely, passing at small \(N\) is possible with favorable outcomes. A percentile bootstrap on all ties can also produce a misleading zero-width interval.

At \(N=500,q=.05\), a **−3 pp** lower-bound test has about 85% normal-approximation power at equality. Adding the **−1 pp point** condition gives about 84% under the same assumptions. Five hundred LongMemEval items across six categories do **not** give 500 independent `single-session-user` controls. Size and report that cohort separately. Use a paired score or exact matched-pair interval for sparse binary discordance, and cluster-aware uncertainty for shared conversations and related `_abs` variants. Repeated generation measures inference variability; it does not create independent clean items.
