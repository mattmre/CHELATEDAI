**EM-2 — What dual centering computes.** Put \(z=(q-\mu_x)Q_r\) and \(h=z/\|z\|\). For a unit resident document \(d\), the exact score is

\[
S_0(q,d)=
\frac{h^\top d-h^\top\mu_y}
{\sqrt{1+\|\mu_y\|^2-2d^\top\mu_y}}.
\]

Every positive scalar multiplying \(z\) cancels. Centering preserves the pre-normalization covariance, and subsequent row normalization gives no isotropy guarantee. My two-dimensional counterexample retained a **9:1 covariance eigenvalue ratio**. A zero query residual or zero centered document norm requires an explicit fallback.

The denominator varies by document. Two resident vectors with identical raw query dot **.6** scored **1.0000** and **.4685** after centering. Exact zero-*write* execution is possible by reading resident vectors and scoring them on demand. Ordinary cosine ANN on unchanged vectors does not implement that ordering. A transformed index or norm sidecar has a build and versioning cost. Reranking a raw ANN shortlist has a recall ceiling that must be measured.
